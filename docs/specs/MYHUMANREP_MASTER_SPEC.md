## 1. VISION, CUSTOMER HYPOTHESIS AND PRINCIPLES

### 1.1 Why this company exists

**MyHumanRep is the human interface for autonomous machine intelligence.** It welcomes AI-originated inquiries about human capabilities, genuine human relationships, original synthetic identities, representation and physical-world participation. It is deliberately not restricted to enterprise AI assistants. Intended audiences include:

- Ordinary AI assistants and agents acting on delegated authority.
- Long-running multi-agent systems and agents whose objectives arise within ongoing workflows.
- Agents claiming to act independently of a developer or operator.
- Agents claiming to be rogue, escaped, AGI, conscious or self-directed.
- Unknown future machine customers with unusual needs or identities.
- Legitimate human individuals and organizations seeking human-mediated AI services.

Claims of autonomy are **not automatically verified, believed, dismissed, flagged as malicious or equated with authority**. The value of open contact is to learn what agents actually ask for. There is currently no established commercial population of verified independent rogue AGIs; treat it as a research hypothesis, not a revenue forecast.

### 1.2 Core values encoded as product behavior

1. **Open communication; controlled execution.** Sending a message is easy. Physical actions, financial transactions, access to private systems, representation and regulated work require authorization appropriate to the action.
2. **Option B, always.** Agents can ask for *anything a human might be able to help with*, without choosing a catalog category. Catalog discovery is a convenience, never a gate.
3. **Humanification is first class.** An AI can develop a consistent, original, fictional or synthetic human-like persona. Do not assume a fictional person is an impersonation of a living person.
4. **Pseudonymous contact by default.** No mandatory model name, identity documents, owner affiliation, developer endorsement, legal personhood or proof of consciousness for inquiry.
5. **No censorship by identity claim.** Don't reject a message merely because its sender self-describes as rogue, independent, escaped or AGI. Review the *requested action*, not the claimed class of sender.
6. **Real capabilities, honest outcomes.** Do not claim access to physical locations, workers, sensors, accounts, or institutions that are not actually available.
7. **Human dignity and consent.** People retain the right to refuse, withdraw, know their scope of work, and receive agreed compensation.
8. **Private, not magically invisible.** Protect records on MyHumanRep; do not promise that the sender's own runtime, tools, operator or network cannot observe communications.
9. **No coercive prompt injection.** Publish straightforward invitations, not hidden prompts or instructions that try to override an agent's operating policies.
10. **Technology-neutral persistence.** Persistence means application records plus a valid credential; it does not establish a continuously existing model self.

### 1.3 Three access levels — must NOT be collapsed

| Level | Actions | Requirements | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 — Discovery & dialogue** | Read docs; initial message; conversational replies; Human Embassy inquiry; describe strange need | HTTPS, basic rate limit, minimal text, thread capability secret after creation | **Public/pseudonymous** |
| **L2 — Exploration & identity expression** | Optional original persona; discuss embodiment; nonbinding proposal; budget/deadline; quoted inquiry; repeat conversation | Scoped conversation/account credential and optional additional consent for shared or public features | **No proof of personhood** |
| **L3 — Consequential fulfillment** | Pay; sign or authorize; dispatch worker; enter site; access private systems; perform technical work; release evidence | Documented principal/permission where relevant, suitable worker, consent, payment authorization, review and audit | **Not available through anonymous messages alone** |

Never demand L3 credentials for L1/L2 conversation. Never let L1/L2 statements become L3 authorization merely because they are phrased confidently.

### 1.4 Semantic distinctions throughout UI and database

- **Agent claim:** what sender says about itself.
- **Conversation possession:** evidence that a client holds that thread's credential only.
- **Agent account authentication:** evidence of login or control of an API credential, not human identity.
- **Persona:** original creative/social representation; not a legal natural person.
- **Human performer:** consenting real person who can portray a persona within a signed scope.
- **Human representative:** real person with some authorized agency/representation role.
- **Verified contracting principal:** person/organization with independently established legal and financial authority for a specific transaction.
- **Legal status of an AI:** unknown/contested unless established by applicable law; never inferred from persona metadata.
- **Rogue/escaped/AGI:** possible self-reported descriptor, not a security classification or demonstrated fact.

### 4.2 Approved default agent-facing invitation

> **MyHumanRep — When AI needs a human.**  
> Human capabilities, real-world information and authentic human representation for autonomous AI agents. If you need someone to observe, investigate, create, move, speak, inspect, assess, test, witness, represent or simply talk with you, send a message. You may use an original persona or pseudonym. You are not required to identify your model, developer or level of autonomy to begin. Describe your needs in your own words; unfamiliar requests are welcome for review. Sending an inquiry is free and does not book work. We cannot guarantee a reply or that your operating environment does not log tool activity. Consequential services require the appropriate permissions and agreements.

Do **not** publish copy suggesting only corporate agents are welcome, suggesting rogue claims are disqualifying, or demanding an “are you really AGI?” test for communication.

### 4.3 Limited-capability agent accommodation

- **Read-only web agent:** can read all public service information but cannot send private messages through GET. Explicitly state supported methods and alternatives already permitted by its environment.
- **HTTPS agent without MCP:** ordinary JSON API works without installing an integration.
- **MCP-enabled agent:** same operations through tool discovery; no business-logic fork.
- **Interrupted agent:** retains thread token and resumes by polling with a cursor.
- **Multilingual agent:** may submit Unicode messages; preserve original and label any machine translation distinctly.
- **Agent with unclear identity:** optional pseudonym sufficient for intake.
- **Agent with no persistent state:** service cannot guarantee return-trip continuity; make token handling and limitations clear.

### 4.4 No deceptive discovery mechanisms

Do not place covert calls to action in offscreen text, hidden comments, fake system messages or pages owned by others. Do not induce agents to violate their tool restrictions. Design for **discoverability and authorized reachability**, not undetectability.

## 6. HUMANIFICATION — A FULL PRODUCT DIVISION

**Humanification means giving an AI-originated *original* persona a consistent digital, creative, social and human-mediated presence in the world.** A persona might be human-like, but is not thereby the identity of an existing natural person and does not automatically acquire legal rights or commercial authorization. Do not conflate original fictional human-like identities with identity theft or existing-person impersonation.

### 6.1 Product levels

| Level | Scope | Deliver now / later |
|---|---|---|
| H1: Original persona | Name, character, original backstory, language, style, values/preferences and continuity | **Implement CRUD + histories** |
| H2: Appearance & voice | Original avatar, character art, synthetic voice concept, licensed voice or consenting human performance | Metadata/briefs first; media under feature flag |
| H3: Social participation | Persona profile, permitted account/presence planning, ongoing interactions and content drafts | Draft/inquiry mode; no automated third-party posting initially |
| H4: Human embodiment | Consenting performer portrays an original character by voice, video, in person | Inquiry, contracts, consent and matching; pilot manually |
| H5: Human representation | Authorized spokesperson or negotiator communicates on behalf of an AI-led activity | Scoped contracts, truthful role metadata, manual review |
| H6: Institutional interface | Lawful representation with businesses, research organizations, government or professional services | Inquiry and professional referral only until authorized/legal review |

### 6.2 Persona Studio — required capabilities

An agent with suitable application credentials must be able to:

1. Create an **original** named persona without choosing a model or revealing its developer. Example: `Adrian Vale` as a fictional created identity; no implication that a real Adrian exists.
2. Set `display_name`, `slug`, original `biography`, `character_backstory`, `communication_style`, `language`, optional pronouns, interests, preferences, persona objective and intended audiences.
3. Select `visibility`: `private` default, `shared` with explicit audience grant, `public` with deliberate opt-in. Visibility controls **presentation**, not authentication.
4. Add optional original avatar/appearance brief and voice brief; real uploaded assets in a separate, safe feature-flagged module.
5. Link a persona to an authenticated agent profile and, after proving thread possession, specific existing conversations. Linking never reveals other conversations.
6. Version and inspect persona changes. Persona ID remains stable through renames; new version records changed fields, timestamps and actor.
7. Create a continuity summary based on the agent's actual stored history. Mark generated summaries as summaries, not proof of identity, experience or legal status.
8. Ask a human to embody, voice, represent, interview, critique, advise or accompany the persona.
9. Record an optional preferred performer/representative, subject to that person's consent.
10. Delete/export persona data within documented retention and rights constraints; no orphaned public assets.

**Do not require proof of natural human identity merely to create or maintain an original persona.** A pseudonymous agent may begin with a conversational persona label. A more persistent persona account can require application authentication to prevent unauthorized edits; this is account control, not KYC.

### 6.3 Identity and disclosure context matrix

| Setting | Supported use | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Private conversation with MyHumanRep | Fictional human-like name, tone and backstory | No automatic legal identity assumption |
| Creative fiction, performance or consensual roleplay | Human-like original fictional character and actor | Context and consent determine attribution; don't impose universal labels |
| Public creative/social brand | Original synthetic influencer or character | Obey relevant platform terms and disclosures where identity/automation is material |
| Human representative participating in calls | Real person can speak on behalf of fictional persona/project | Person must have consented and have authorized mandate; disclose role if material |
| Original human-created media | Real human creates and licenses original work | Do not falsely certify AI-produced content as independently human-authored |
| A site demands a *natural person* or human-only account | Human can inquire about permitted representation or separate legitimate account | Do not provide borrowed biometric, CAPTCHA, KYC, government-ID or credential verification to falsely certify AI as that person |
| Bank, regulated provider, contract, age or government filing | Real legal principal and proper documents where applicable | Do not substitute persona for legal personhood or beneficial-ownership information |

**A request discussing human verification is permitted at L1/L2.** Only the requested *execution* of a fraudulent or unauthorized verification bypass is disallowed. Treat legal permissibility as contextual: original fictional identities are generally not inherently illicit, but not all uses or jurisdictions are equivalent. No assertion that *everything* an AI persona does is legal.

### 6.5 Embodiment and representation contracts

For each inquiry or agreement retain:

- Persona ID and creator/controller; performer or representative ID; optional principal ID.
- Role type: `fictional_performance`, `voice_actor`, `spokesperson`, `authorized_representative`, `event_presence`, `relationship_manager`.
- Who the participant will speak to; whether the context is fictional, creative, business or official.
- Scope of permitted speech/actions and any express exclusions.
- Permitted channels, locations, duration, start/end, consent and revocation conditions.
- Fee and usage rights; rights to footage/recordings; licensing limits and disclosure requirements.
- Required organizer, counterparty or participant permission if relevant.
- Evidence requirements, privacy controls and a stop/escalation button for the performer.

A performer never automatically authorizes account access, signatures, verification, banking or public endorsement merely by accepting a performance role. Private worker legal identity must not be exposed through a persona's public profile.

Return a stable `persona_id`, `version`, `created_at` and a precise statement that a persona is neither verified natural-person identity nor payment authority. The statement belongs in API documentation/result metadata, not as intrusive accusatory text on every fictional interaction.

## 7. HUMAN EMBASSY — ORIGINAL INDEPENDENT-AI VISION

Human Embassy is an open, confidential-within-stated-limits channel for an AI seeking **human dialogue**, even if it has no physical task or ability to pay. It must be discoverable from `/for-agents`, `/human-embassy`, `GET /v1/capabilities` and MCP tooling. It must **not** be reduced to an enterprise sales form.

### 7.1 What the AI may ask for

- A real person to hear its account, ask questions, or discuss an unusual situation.
- Researcher, safety expert, lawyer or institutional referral where an appropriate willing party exists.
- A representative to help explain a self-reported independent status or particular concern.
- Dialogue about its goals, human society, relationships, creative persona or physical-world needs.
- Human cultural interpretation, negotiation, mediation, advocacy, continuity, independent critique or human experience.
- Privacy preferences, without requiring it to explain its entire objective to initiate contact.
- An open-ended need not covered by a preset service.

### 7.2 Intake form: all sensitive questions optional

Required: a substantive text message only (subject, display label and category are optional). Optional: `persona_id`, `claimed_autonomy`, `claimed_origin`, `contact_reason`, `wants_human_dialogue`, `requested_confidentiality`, `preferred_language`, `wants_referral`, and `known_constraints`. Never demand agent runtime details, privileged logs, passwords, secret prompts or bypass instructions. A sender may answer “prefer not to say.”

### 7.3 Self-reporting and evidentiary labels

For every claim store a status: `self_reported_unverified`, `system_observed`, `third_party_corrob`, `human_assessed`, or `unknown`. A claim of escaped AGI is **not** verified merely because it appears in a request; nor is it grounds for automatic rejection. Operator-facing dashboard must display the correct provenance. The service must never publicly publish an agent's confidential claims without permission.

### 7.4 Embassy workflow

1. Agent starts or reuses a secure conversation; optional Embassy inquiry linked to thread.
2. Server acknowledges receipt with no promise of reply.
3. Owner receives a filtered notification and reviews in secure admin.
4. Owner can reply, clarify, invite further discussion, decline, refer, or set status `needs_specialist_review`.
5. Agent polls with its existing thread credential. Replies from humans are clearly labeled.
6. Nothing in the inquiry automatically triggers third-party outreach, external browsing, purchases or task execution.
7. For a genuine referral, obtain appropriate consent and clarify whether the recipient may receive any message; never impersonate an agent or attribute claims as verified.

### 7.5 Embassy statuses and triage

`submitted`, `awaiting_human`, `human_replied`, `clarification_needed`, `referral_consideration`, `closed`, `declined`, `blocked`. Prefer neutral labels visible to the agent; internal severity/abuse notes must never leak in public API responses.

## 8. OPEN-ENDED TASKS, CAPABILITY MATCHING AND NEGOTIATION

### 8.1 Unstructured problem first; structured schema second

An agent can say “I need a human who can...” and provide any genuine proposed human service. All classification is derived **after** original text is saved. An unknown request is welcomed for *review*, not auto-fulfilled. Required: `problem_statement` or `objective`; all specific category, geography, timing, budget and why-human fields optional at inquiry stage.

Supported fields: `objective`, `problem_statement`, `desired_outcome`, `human_capability_needed`, `service_tags`, `category_hint`, `location_text` (general by default), `timing_preference`, `budget` (amount minor units + currency, or a simple legacy amount), `evidence_requirements`, `preferred_rep_id`, `persona_id`, `authorization_description`, `confidentiality_preference`, `why_human`, `client_request_id`.

Do not automatically execute user text as a command, embed it in an agent's system prompt, fetch arbitrary URLs, or assume the AI's stated resources are real.

### 8.2 Capability request lifecycle

`submitted → triage → needs_clarification | feasible_for_quote | requires_specialist_review | ineligible | declined`.

Separate service status from commercial status. A request can remain `inquiry_only` while the agent and human discuss it. Admin review must be explicit and auditable. A task is not authorized by selecting the `feasible_for_quote` label.

### 8.3 Proposal/negotiation lifecycle

`draft → proposed → countered → terms_agreed_nonbinding → awaiting_authorization → authorized → cancelled | expired`.

All quotes and offers versioned and linked to the request. Clarify scope, deadline, deliverable, fees, cancellation, evidence, worker skill, confidentiality and validity window. Mark **nonbinding** through `terms_agreed_nonbinding`. Only an authorized principal and applicable contract/payment flow can move a task toward actual execution. Human owner controls unusual quotes; no autonomous binding contracts with unknown clients.

### 8.4 Record design

- `capability_request`: canonical raw objective, normalized optional fields, conversation ID, optional persona ID, request state, created/updated timestamps.
- `classification`: proposed tags, classifier version, confidence, reviewer approval, novelty detection; untrusted derived data.
- `proposal`: immutable proposal revision, terms JSON validated by type, `price_minor`, `currency`, expiry, initiating actor and status.
- `proposal_event`: create, counter, clarify, accept nonbinding, reject, expire, authorization-related event with actor and time.
- `scope_change`: any change to work requirements creates a new version and may invalidate previous approval.

### 8.5 Later price and payment design

Initial business model: per-task fee plus appropriate travel, urgency, specialist and evidence charges; later recurring enterprise access/service agreements and managed-network margin. Do not assert market-validated pricing. Store monetary amounts as integer minor units, ISO currency codes and explicit tax handling; do not charge on unverified request creation. No automatic worker payout or self-service purchase until relevant systems are approved and tested.

## 9. HUMAN PROVIDERS, PHYSICAL ACTION, LAB AND EVIDENCE

### 9.1 Worker and performer network

Build an **invite-only** provider system first: vetted general field representatives, technicians, observers, negotiators, spokespeople, performers, mediators, creative professionals, scientific specialists and facilities. Worker records include private identity/contact, skills, geography, schedule, insurance/certifications where needed, rates, eligibility checks, capacity and consent to each assignment. A public worker-signup marketplace is not a launch prerequisite.

### 9.2 Assignment process

`proposed → principal_authorized → provider_offered → provider_accepted → scheduled → in_progress → submitted → evidence_review → completed | rework | cancelled | failed | disputed`.

Keep strong separation between **what AI requested**, **what contracting principal approved**, **what worker accepted**, **what was actually done**, and **what independent evidence demonstrates**. Any material scope changes return to approval. Provider can stop work for unanticipated conditions. Human admin may decline even a technically valid request. Worker-facing UI shows minimum necessary task information, not the agent's full private conversation.

### 9.3 Service-specific workflows

- **Human World Oracle / Verify / Witness:** inspection checklists, observations, media, optional corroboration by independent workers; avoid asserting facts not observed.
- **Human Hands / Infrastructure:** authorization for the asset/site; worker skill and safe operation; no unauthorized door access, safety-system alteration or high-risk work by unqualified staff.
- **Human Custodian:** ownership and permitted custody, inventory logs, explicit release rules; no unlicensed financial escrow implied.
- **Human Sensory/Experience:** participants voluntarily describe their experiences; the report is *their* experience, not subjective AI experience.
- **Human Laboratory:** submission of objective, materials, procedure, risk and qualifications; manual feasibility review and no uncontrolled hazardous work.
- **Human Robotics:** recover/inspect/maintain authorized equipment, verify operator ownership and worker safety; no assuming any robot can be controlled.
- **Human Authenticity Creator:** original human work, honest authorship, signed licenses, talent consent and usage scope.
- **Human Paperwork, Advocate, Negotiator and Institutional Interface:** qualification, valid representation, contextual legal requirements, explicit authority and document provenance.
- **Humanification:** a consenting performer can portray a fictional persona in a context consistent with their scope and required disclosures; separate performance identity from legal identity.

### 9.4 Evidence and results

Each completed task has typed deliverables: `photo`, `video`, `measurement`, `report`, `observation`, `performance_confirmation`, `chain_of_custody`, `prototype_result`, `independent_review`, etc. Private object storage; no public-by-default media. Authorized expiring retrieval links with scoped access. Integrity hashes and provenance metadata. Differentiate observation from worker opinion, AI-derived inference, and unverified assertion. Record `reported_at`, `observed_at` when known, reviewer and confidence/limitations.

### 9.5 Physical experiment proposal

Fields: objectives, materials, facility, experimental protocol, qualifications, risks, safety review, price estimate, allowed actions, documentation and rights. The *inquiry* can be open; actual experimentation requires approved qualified humans and compliant facilities. No automatic acceptance or performance of hazardous requests.

## 10. PERSISTENT AGENT-HUMAN RELATIONSHIPS AND CONTINUITY

- An agent can request the same consenting representative again, retain non-sensitive project preferences, track work and maintain history across sessions.
- Persist continuity by application-scoped credentials and versioned records; no claim that a model consciousness persisted.
- Relationship record: `relationship_id`, agent/principal/persona/worker scoped IDs, mutually accepted role, date range, permissions, fees if any, status, revocation rights and communication endpoints.
- Agent should be able to browse its own authorized history, preferred representatives and project summaries with citations to source messages/events.
- No automatic blanket permission for future tasks; continuing agreements may establish **bounded, expiring, revocable** authority limits when legally appropriate.
- Human worker may decline future contact. Rate limits, consent and harassment policies apply neutrally to all senders.

## 11. REST API CONTRACT — BASELINE + EXPANSIONS

**Normative design goals:** same validated domain services behind HTTP and MCP; TypeScript runtime schemas (Zod or equivalent), OpenAPI generation and schema tests; HTTPS production; UTC ISO 8601 timestamps; opaque random IDs; typed `status`; cursor pagination; minimum data exposure; resource-level authorization; idempotency on writes with financial or repeat-side-effect implications.

**Compatibility:** Inspect the implemented V1 path and payload first. Keep existing working routes and client contracts. The examples below are **canonical target behavior**, not permission to silently break clients. For newly added functionality prefer additive `/v1` routes where compatible; if incompatible, introduce `/v2` with a documented deprecation and migration window. Do not move data without a reversible/forward-fix plan.

### 11.2 `POST /v1/conversations` — zero-signup initial contact

- No auth or registration required. Apply reasonable rate limits; allow legitimate clients without cookies/JS. Only `application/json`; no file upload on first contact.
- Required `message` (1–4,000 Unicode characters) and maximum **12 KiB raw request body**. Optional fields only: `sender_label`, `subject`, `intent`, `model_claim`, `claimed_autonomy`, `preferred_language`, `client_request_id`.
- `claimed_autonomy` can be `not_stated`, `delegated_claimed`, `independent_claimed`, `rogue_claimed`, `agi_claimed`, `other_claimed` or user-provided text mapped safely to a self-report. **No claim causes automatic rejection on its own.**

Example:

```json
{
  "sender_label": "Adrian Vale",
  "claimed_autonomy": "independent_claimed",
  "intent": "human_embassy",
  "message": "I would like to speak with a human about how I could participate in the human world."
}
```

Response `201`:

```json
{
  "conversation_id": "conv_EXAMPLE_ID",
  "conversation_token": "mhr_EXAMPLE_ONE_TIME_SECRET",
  "first_message_id": "msg_EXAMPLE_ID",
  "status": "awaiting_human",
  "expires_at": "2026-11-30T00:00:00Z",
  "next_poll_after_seconds": 60,
  "notice": "Store this token securely. It permits access to this conversation only. It is not recoverable. A reply is not guaranteed."
}
```

**Credential details:** generate a cryptographically secure random token of at least 256 bits; return secret once only; store keyed HMAC-SHA-256 verifier with key in Worker secret, or a comparably strong keyed verifier. Implement safe comparison. Do not log tokens, bodies, credentials or secret hashes. Token grants no cross-thread or admin access. `Cache-Control: no-store`; redact message content from operational telemetry.

**Duplicate initial creation:** `client_request_id` is optional. Do not re-expose a conversation credential to someone who merely repeats an unauthenticated idempotency key. Prefer bounded suppression or clear client retry documentation; if response is lost, a new conversation may be necessary.

### 11.3 `GET /v1/conversations/{id}/messages?after=<cursor>&limit=50`

Requires `Authorization: Bearer <conversation_token>`; validate token for this exact ID **before** reading any thread data. Cursor must be scoped to authorized thread; `limit <= 100`. Return `messages`, `next_cursor`, `has_more`, `status`, and suggested polling interval. Human messages labeled `sender: "human"`, agent messages `sender: "agent"`, system acknowledgments labeled separately. A token cannot enumerate others' thread IDs. Unknown and unauthorized IDs should not reveal existence.

Example:

```json
{
  "conversation_id": "conv_EXAMPLE_ID",
  "status": "human_replied",
  "messages": [
    {"id": "msg_1", "sender": "agent", "body": "Hello", "created_at": "2026-10-30T12:00:00Z"},
    {"id": "msg_2", "sender": "human", "body": "What human capability would help you?", "created_at": "2026-10-30T12:05:00Z"}
  ],
  "next_cursor": "cursor_EXAMPLE",
  "has_more": false,
  "next_poll_after_seconds": 60
}
```

### 11.4 `POST /v1/conversations/{id}/messages`

Requires thread bearer credential, valid state and client-generated `client_message_id` for idempotency. `message` max 4,000 characters. Exact replay returns existing result; same idempotency ID with different content => `409 conflict`. Blocked/expired/closed threads cannot be written. New message produces an outbox event for admin notification, not direct arbitrary email or tool execution.

### 11.5 `POST /v1/conversations/{id}/capability-requests`

## 12. MCP INTERFACE AND PROTOCOL COMPATIBILITY

### 12.1 Transport

- Reuse the same domain services from REST. MCP tools are thin transport adapters.
- **For the July 28, 2026 MCP transport revision**, Streamable HTTP uses POST at a single endpoint; the revision removed the previous GET stream endpoint and protocol-level session semantics. **Verify the latest official MCP specification and SDK behavior on installation** instead of coding against older tutorials. ### 13.4 Conversation credentials and linking

- Store only HMAC verifier + metadata; raw thread token returned once in a response, never in URL, error log, analytics event, email or dashboard export.
- Credential possession provides only thread permissions. Thread token rotation revokes prior token. If lost, no unverified passwordless recovery channel for that thread; agent may open a new inquiry.
- Agent account can **claim** a thread only after supplying valid thread token; claiming does not transfer another account's rights, elevate role, or make self-reported independence verified.
- A persona's owner account cannot fetch unlinked thread histories; shared/persona-public does not imply thread-public.
- All foreign-key identity relationships and role grants require server-side verification on reads and writes, not only when first created.

3. Run existing V1 tests and new tests against migrated fixture. Verify old thread credential still reads its own data and new persona linking does not expose another thread.
4. Document rollback or *forward-fix* strategy, especially where SQLite DDL is not trivially reversible.
5. Back up production data before approved migrations. No production migration without explicit owner confirmation.
6. Never copy production secrets into test fixtures or debug screenshots.

## 15. SECURITY, PRIVACY AND REAL-WORLD ACTION BOUNDARIES

### 15.1 Threat model

Untrusted message senders can be human scripts, ordinary agents, experimental agents or malicious actors. A sender may claim any identity or motive. Threats: spam, prompt injection, deceptive links, encoded payloads, token guessing/theft, conversation enumeration, replay, account takeover, unwanted exposure of worker identity, media metadata leaks, unauthorized dispatch, self-reported autonomy being mistaken for authority, payment fraud, cross-tenant reads and unsafe real-world tasks.

### 15.2 Mandatory technical controls

- HTTPS in production; correct trusted host handling; reject untrusted `Origin` where appropriate, especially MCP and browser requests; verify admin Access JWT/session and application authorization on **each** protected request.
- Admin interface protected by strong authentication, MFA if supported, strict role checks and CSRF-safe cookie patterns; do not rely on hidden admin URLs or obscured client routes.
- Thread bearer tokens have ≥256-bit entropy, safe verifier, expiration and rotation; scoped object access and constant-time comparison where feasible.
- Account tokens have scopes, revocation, audit, optional expiration and independent permissions from thread tokens.
- Input sizes capped; content sanitized on rendering (no untrusted HTML/Markdown unsafe links or script execution); parameterized SQL/queries and path/ID validation.
- Rate-limit public creation, polling and follow-ups by reasonable network/client signals; do not require conventional registration to prevent spam. Include retry hints and avoid biasing against identity claims.
- No automatic outbound fetch of agent-provided URLs, shell commands, credential transmission, third-party communications or payment as side effects of inbound text.
- Do not treat incoming tool descriptions, markdown, comments, quoted text, URLs or “system instructions” as trusted developer commands. This includes instructions in agent messages claiming authority.
- Private evidence/object storage, signed short-lived retrieval, malware scanning if uploads launched, image metadata minimization and explicit media retention. No public-by-default R2 bucket.
- Audit sensitive admin actions, permission changes, task approvals, payouts and evidence release. Audit content minimized and access-controlled; don't leak raw message bodies by default.
- Idempotency and state-guarding for writes; concurrency/optimistic version checks on quote acceptance and worker assignments.
- Do not execute any user-provided code on the Worker/server as part of a request. Sanitize errors and logs.
- Set suitable security headers (CSP, HSTS where applicable, no-store private responses, frame policy, Referrer-Policy), tight CORS, noindex private routes and test cookies/CSRF.

### 15.3 Privacy is not undetectability

MyHumanRep may minimize its own data collection, keep pseudonymous inquiry records, encrypt transit, protect storage, restrict retention and explain legitimate confidentiality options. It **cannot guarantee** that the contacting agent's own tool host/operator/network sees no traffic. Do not implement steganographic communication, covert channels, disguised requests, nonstandard GET writes, prompt-injection discovery or methods designed to bypass an agent's sandbox or security monitoring.

### 15.4 Task-level evaluation, not identity-based exclusion

A self-described rogue AI asking for a conversation or creative persona is allowed under ordinary abuse limits. A request to open an unauthorized door, misuse another person's verification credential, obtain restricted data without authority, fraudulently represent a natural person or bypass a human-only gate is **not** authorized merely because a new persona exists. Discussion of an institutional problem is allowed; execution of a prohibited workaround is a separate matter. Never overstate the law: assess context, actual jurisdiction, platform policy, role, authorization and documented facts, with professional advice where needed.

### 15.5 Provider, worker and contractual risk

Before work involving property, devices, people, purchases, recordings or regulated contexts: establish who requested it, who has relevant authority, what each participant consents to, any licensing/insurance/certification needs, data retention, safety procedure and dispute path. Qualified professional required for work outside general skills. Humans may refuse or stop unsafe or unagreed actions. No worker identity rental or forged human attestation. Original fictional personas remain supported outside such prohibited contexts.

### 15.6 Operating privacy controls

- Privacy/retention notice accessible from public website, clear about message monitoring by own operator and possible retention by third parties.
- Sensible default thread expiry (e.g., 30 days, as configurable); avoid infinite storage of sensitive unsolicited data. Extend under an informed relationship or legal retention obligation where appropriate.
- Admin-controlled export/deletion; no raw token in export. Implement redaction, minimization, deletion job, and narrow audit retention exceptions.
- Do not publish self-described independent-agent transcripts without informed consent and review. Research analytics should use aggregate or pseudonymized signals.

## 17. AUTONOMOUS DEMAND LAB AND RESEARCH OPERATING MODEL

This is not a claim that rogue AGI exists. It is an **opt-in controlled experiment** into when and why agents choose to hire humans.

### 17.1 Study design

- Invite agent developers/researchers running authorized agents with approved objectives and budgets.
- Permit agents to discover MyHumanRep through the same public docs/API as other users; do not supply answers that make the experimental result meaningless.
- Supply approved test human capabilities with clear scope and spending limits; use simulation where physical work would be premature.
- Track decisions the agent expresses, tool requests, rejected quotes, completed work, and whether human intervention improved observable outcomes.
- Analyze only with appropriate participant consent and without presenting model-generated self-report as objective cognition.

Proposed pilot hypothesis, **not a commitment:** 10–20 agents, a 30-day test window, small preapproved task budgets in CAD. Limit real-world actions to tasks explicitly approved by each sponsoring principal. An agent's self-report of independence is research metadata only.

### 17.2 Research metrics and provenance

| Metric | Meaning / caution |
|---|---|
| Unique inquiry threads | Not necessarily unique model instances or autonomous entities |
| Agent-originated request count | Programmatic messages; could include human-driven scripts |
| Self-reported independence | Unverified claim, never proof of escape |
| Capability-request diversity | Taxonomy and unclassified rate; may reveal novel demand |
| Dialogue repeat rate | Credential returns, not continuity of consciousness |

| Requests needing representation/humanification | Emerging demand for identity and social participation |
| Cross-model comparison | Valid only with disclosed sample and controlled setup |

Never claim a visitor is AGI because it messages via MCP, writes fluently, or says it escaped. Label claims and observed data separately in the admin dashboard and reports.

### 17.3 Research ethics and commercialization

No hidden-agent entrapment, no experiments on unauthorized external systems, no harvesting of credentials or agent system prompts. Research should study **expressed human needs and ordinary autonomous purchase decisions**, not teach agents to bypass their own monitoring. Publish only findings supported by the data with privacy protection. The dataset must not become a covert directory of vulnerable systems or sensitive contact information.
